About Straite to Hale

We specialize in immersive set design for haunted mazes, attractions, and events — outdoor and indoor scenery, puzzle hardware, animatronic gags, and finish work that holds up to guests and cameras.

Outdoor Sets

Facades, queue barns, and exterior scenes engineered for weather, night lighting, and high throughput. Modular thinking for strike and storage between seasons.

Indoor Sets

Walkthrough rooms, corridors, and dense scenic layers built for sightlines, scare timing, and actor paths — tuned for both guest experience and crew operations.

Escape Room Puzzles

Story-led puzzle props, reset-friendly mechanisms, and themed integrations that stay reliable night after night.

Animatronics

Pop figures, simple motion beats, and reveal gags coordinated with audio and lighting so the moment reads clearly in the maze.

Custom Paint & Textures

Aging, stone, wood grain, metal corrosion, and scenic treatments sized for guest proximity and photo/video capture.

Concept to Install

Design support, fabrication, delivery, on-site install, and tune-up visits so the environment is show-ready when doors open.
